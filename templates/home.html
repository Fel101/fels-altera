        Write a companion

        A companion keeps memory, notes and a story, and grows with you. Everything here is what they know about themselves; you can change it later in their settings.

        Only a name and an age are needed. Ages are 18 or older, and the text passes the same safety rules as an imported companion.

        Kids mode

        Kids mode is a guardrail, not a certification. It is not a parental-control product and it has not been tested or approved as one.

        What it does, for a pal in kids mode

        Tells the model it is talking with a child and to keep to child-friendly topics. Answers messages about self-harm, sex and romance, drugs and alcohol, violence, gambling or personal details with a fixed reply instead of the model, and points to a trusted grown-up. Checks each reply before it is shown and replaces one that strays. Turns off web search, pictures, check-ins and other messages the pal would start itself.

        What it cannot do

        It works from word lists and instructions, so it will miss some things, and the model can still get things wrong. It does not stop anyone opening other people on this device, or changing these settings. A grown-up still needs to keep an eye on things.
