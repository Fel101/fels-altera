# Responsible use

Fel's Studio helps you record voices with permission and make fictional characters. Altera lets you talk with those
characters, give them voices and make pictures with them.

## Keep companions fictional

Make fictional adults. Avoid real people's identities and likenesses, including celebrities and look-alikes.
Never use a character or voice to impersonate, deceive or harass someone. Review generated pictures and speech
before sharing them. For Altera's **Picture of us** feature, use only your own photo, and only if you are an adult
and agree to send it to the services shown. [Making pictures](AVATARS.md#picture-of-us)

## Record and clone with consent

Use your own voice or a recording whose speaker has clearly agreed to the specific use. Get permission in writing
and keep it with the recording. Record where the audio came from, what the speaker allows and whether they agree
to sharing the recording or the synthetic voice. A voice from a public video, stream or podcast still needs permission.

A character card can include a recording used to clone a voice. Review its source and allowed use before you import
it into Voice Lab. Share a card with that recording only if the permission covers sharing it. Keep recordings and
consent records private and remove copies you no longer need.

## Read the licence badges

The model and the recording can each set terms for what you make. Check the original terms for your use.
Altera shows the licence rating supplied by Voice Lab for a voice and by NPC Forge for a character card.

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Commercial use allowed** | The recorded terms allow commercial use. Keep any required notices. |
| **Allowed, with conditions** | Read the conditions before you publish, sell or host the work. |
| **Not cleared** | A licence or permission is missing or unclear. Treat commercial use as unconfirmed. |
| **Non-commercial only** | The recorded terms limit the work to non-commercial uses. |

Altera's MIT licence covers its own code. [Licences for models, voices and packages](README.md#licensing)

## Label what you share

Altera marks generated chat pictures **Imagined image** and shows a card's **AI-generated** label when the card
supplies one. Keep the supplied labels and tell people when a picture or voice is AI-generated.

## Add-ons and Kids mode

The separate 18+ add-on is for adults only. Altera's core safety rules apply to it. Kids mode and the add-on
cannot be used in the same installation. Kids mode is a chat guardrail. Use parental-control software and adult
supervision alongside it. [Kids mode](KIDS_MODE.md) · [How Altera keeps things safe](SAFETY.md)
