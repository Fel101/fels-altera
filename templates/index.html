About Altera

            
Altera is a companion that lives on your own computer. Your conversations, memories and pictures stay in its folder, and it talks to the chat model you choose: one running on this PC, or a cloud one if you set it up.

            
Part of Fel's Studio. Four free apps that run on your own PC and work together.
                Voice Slate records voices with consent,
                Voice Lab designs and clones voices,
                NPC Forge makes characters for games, and
                Altera lets you talk with them. Each one also works on its own.

            
Version {{ altera_version }}. Free software under the MIT licence, © 2026 Pete Hudson. It is built on other people's open work, such as Flask, ChromaDB, Whisper and Kokoro; their licences are listed in docs/README.md.

            
Altera is not a person and not a therapist. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a local helpline.

Turn on adult mode?

            
Adult mode allows romantic and sensual conversation and pictures, and, at the highest level, explicit ones. It is for adults only.

            
            
Some things stay off at every level: anything involving minors or anyone childlike, anything involving real people, anything non-consensual. At Suggestive and above, a picture is only delivered after a vision model has checked that the person looks like an adult.

            

                
                
            

        

    


    
    

        

            

                
Companions and pictures

                
            

            
Every companion is a person you can talk to: one made in NPC Forge, or one you wrote here. Altera uses the portrait as the reference for every picture, so pictures keep the same face; a companion with no portrait yet shows an initial, and pictures need a portrait first. Pictures you approve in NPC Forge's studio are fetched automatically and sent instantly, without making anything new. Removing someone deletes their chat, notes, memories and pictures here (NPC Forge's copy is not touched).
